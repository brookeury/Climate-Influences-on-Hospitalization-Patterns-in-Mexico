# Climate-Influences-on-Hospitalization-Patterns-in-Mexico

This directory contains the code for conducting seasonality analyses (wavelet power spectrum, Fourier transformation) and identifying meteorological drivers (fixed-effects and generalized additive models) of hospitalizations across ICD categories, using a country-wide dataset of public hospitalizations in Mexico.

More details can be found in the 2026 publication:
Ury B, Stamper AR, Baker RE. Environmental Research Communications. "Climate Influences on Hospitalization Patterns in Mexico: Evidence from 30 Million Records" doi: https://doi.org/10.1088/2515-7620/ae37d9

To replicate the results and run the scripts, the following data is required:

The Subsistema Automatizado de Egresos Hospitalarios (SAEH) dataset, published by the Sistema Nacional de Información en Salud (SINAIS) of the Mexican Department of Health. The data can be found here: http://www.dgis.salud.gob.mx/contenidos/sinais/s_saeh.html

Temperature data is available from the North American Regional Reanalysis (NARR) model published by the National Centers for Environmental Prediction (NCEP): https://psl.noaa.gov/data/gridded/data.narr.html

Precipitation data is available from the Climate Hazards Group InfraRed Precipitation with Station data (CHIRPS) model published by the Climate Hazard Center at the University of California, Santa Barbara: https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps
